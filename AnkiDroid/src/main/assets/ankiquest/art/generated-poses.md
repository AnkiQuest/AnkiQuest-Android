These Anki-tan pictures are derivatives of Shigeyuki's Anki-tan asset
pack v1.0, made by AnkiQuest and licensed CC BY-SA 4.0: the full-size
asleep, happy, streak, streak_hot, streak_blazing, sad, frozen, frozen_2,
frozen_3, neglected and abandoned poses, the deck_conquered, freeze and
freeze_used moment pictures, and the icon_frozen launcher icon. They were
generated with OpenAI image_gen, which did not expose its model
identifier. The neglected pose has one unlit headband star; its edit
record is archive/docs/ankitan-full-2026-11/neglected-revision.json in
AnkiQuest/AnkiQuest-Art.

Every other Anki-tan picture, including every chibi pose, is a file of
the pack, unchanged.

AnkiQuest/AnkiQuest-Art holds the masters (anki-tan/ and anki-tan/chibi/)
and their generation records. Its manifest.json gives each picture's
licence, origin and credit, and anki-tan/README.md lists which pack file
or record each pose comes from.

Aki's, Ankilope's and Bluma's pictures are AnkiQuest's own, under the
AnkiQuest-Art licence. Some were generated with OpenAI image_gen;
AnkiQuest-Art's generation records say which.

Android WebPs are AnkiQuest-Art's exported copies, from the release
pinned in ankiquest-art.json.
