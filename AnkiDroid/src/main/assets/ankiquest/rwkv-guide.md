**What RWKV does.** RWKV is a small neural network that predicts how well you will remember a card. The RWKV scheduler is experimental and off by default: until you turn it on, nothing about your scheduling changes. On, it can pick which review cards to show you (RWKV-Instant) or set each card's next interval (RWKV-Curve), in the presets you choose. Everything else keeps the scheduler it had.

**Model files.** AnkiQuest ships no RWKV weights: they have no licence for redistribution. Under Settings → AnkiQuest → RWKV model files, Download fetches the model you pick from the JSchoreels/anki-rwkv release on GitHub and checks its SHA-256. Or choose files yourself: the release's zips or the weights.f32.bin files inside them. Importing one size deletes the other sizes' files. The weights are for personal and research use only (WEIGHTS-NOTICE.md of the release).

**Model sizes.** The release has Big, Small and Tiny models, each as a two-head model (for RWKV-Curve and RWKV-Instant) and an Instant-only model that RWKV-Instant uses beside it. Download lists all six with their download size (Big: two-head zip 24.7 MB, Instant-only zip 24.7 MB; Small: two-head zip 3.0 MB, Instant-only zip 4.2 MB; Tiny: two-head zip 4.1 MB, Instant-only zip 4.0 MB) and the memory they need for your collection. The weights.f32.bin files: Big about 11.1 MB, Small about 1.4 MB, Tiny about 1.3 MB. Each card and each note keeps a state: about 26 KB per card and its note for Big, 4.6 KB for Small and 3.1 KB for Tiny, so a 22,000-card collection needs about 0.7 GB with Big and about a quarter of a GB with Small. AnkiQuest uses RWKV only when a quarter of your phone's RAM covers that; a model that does not fit is greyed out with the figures, and its "I know what I'm doing" box lets you download it anyway, though RWKV may then not start. The most accurate model that fits, by the release's published scores, is marked Recommended.

**Turning it on.** Turn on Settings → AnkiQuest → RWKV scheduler, then choose which presets use it on each preset's deck options page: RWKV-Instant to pick your review cards, RWKV-Curve for intervals. Other presets are untouched.

**Turning it off.** Turn the RWKV scheduler switch off and every preset returns to the scheduler you set for it. A preset remembers its RWKV choice; set it back to your usual scheduler on the deck options page to leave RWKV behind.

**The statistics panel.** While RWKV runs, Statistics shows two more cards. RWKV forecast: how many cards are due, near and safe if you stop reviewing now, in an hour, in three hours and by tomorrow. RWKV recall now: each card's predicted recall, in bands, with how many sit below their desired retention.

What the weights are and how they were trained: the [RWKV weights guide](https://github.com/AnkiQuest/AnkiQuest-Anki/blob/master/docs/rwkv-weights.md) of AnkiQuest's Anki fork.
