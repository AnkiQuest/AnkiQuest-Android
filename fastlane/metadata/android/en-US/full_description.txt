AnkiQuest is AnkiDroid, the Android app for the Anki spaced repetition flashcard system, with AnkiQuest built in. Anki's scheduling is unchanged. AnkiQuest adds a study companion, XP, streaks, daily goals and optional social features to keep you studying.

AnkiQuest:
 - Today: study progress, next deck, streak status and daily goal
 - Streaks with streak protection, and a companion whose mood follows your streak
 - XP, levels, records and study time
 - Moments for milestones, level-ups and leaderboard places, to share with friends or other apps
 - Friends and groups, challenges and nudges
 - Friends, Group and Global leaderboards
 - Streak widget

AnkiDroid:
 - Supported card contents: text, images, sounds, video, MathJax
 - Spaced repetition with FSRS
 - Card editor and browser, detailed statistics, night mode
 - Text-to-speech, whiteboard, written answers
 - Syncing with AnkiWeb or your own sync server
 - Import of Anki collections and shared decks

Network services:
AnkiQuest features need an account on an AnkiQuest server. ankiquest.org is the public one. The server is free software (AGPL-3.0) and you can run your own: enter its address when you sign in. When signed in, the app uploads review times, card IDs, intervals, answer times and deck names and progress; it does not upload card text, images or audio. Card labels and deck copies are sent only when you share them. New profiles are public and listed on the global leaderboard; change this in Settings → AnkiQuest → Sharing.

Synchronization is opt-in and uses the non-libre AnkiWeb service by default; it can be pointed at a self-hosted Anki sync server. Crash reports are sent only if you agree.

AnkiQuest installs next to AnkiDroid and has its own collection.
