# Bluma core art

Bluma is an original AnkiQuest purple cow. The face, asleep, happy, streak,
sad, hopeful, frozen and neglected WebPs are generated at build time from
the masters in AnkiQuest/AnkiQuest-Art under cow/ by its tools/art-export.

Prompts, selections and cleanup records are in
docs/branding/bluma-core-2026-11/ in that repository. The images were
generated with OpenAI image_gen, which did not expose its model
identifier. The repository licence applies.
